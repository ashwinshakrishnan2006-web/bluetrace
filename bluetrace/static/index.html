    NEW INVESTIGATION

    Bring your evidence together

    Start with an image. Add AIS and environmental inputs for source screening.

    01   Image and observation

    PNG or JPEG · Max 8 MB and 16 megapixels. The model processes a 200 × 150 grid.

    02   Vessel movement

    Required columns: mmsi, timestamp, latitude, longitude. Optional: name. Use timezone-aware timestamps. Maximum 2 MB or 20,000 rows.

    03   Geographic bounds

    Use accurate north-up image bounds. Missing or invented bounds make attribution unreliable. Extent is limited to 5 degrees.

    Geographic parameters

    04   Environmental assumptions

    Constant east/north velocity components in m/s. Positive values mean eastward/northward. Used only when geographic analysis is enabled.

    Inputs and outputs are saved locally after successful analysis.

    TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

    Model and methodology

    Understand the data, measured results and assumptions behind each result.

    Segmentation baseline

    A random forest classifies pixels using intensity, local mean, local variation and gradient features. Training uses real SAR-derived images with labelled slick masks. Predictions are made on a 200 × 150 grid.

    Dataset: Garcia-INPE Oil Spill Segmentation Dataset. CC BY 4.0. The original 108 / 43 / 22 train, validation and test split is preserved. A decision threshold is chosen on validation data only.

    View dataset source ↗

    Source reconstruction

    Transport = current + windage × wind. Each candidate region is moved backward over nine ages within the entered interval. The radius is 250 m + velocity uncertainty × elapsed seconds. These circles express assumptions; they are not statistical confidence intervals.

    AIS association

    Vessel positions are interpolated only within observed track segments. Gaps above 30 minutes and implied speeds above 25 m/s are rejected. Candidates with very weak spatial agreement are omitted.

    Practical limits

    Low winds, seepage and other dark features can resemble oil. The dataset does not identify responsible vessels. Constant drift cannot capture changing currents, coastlines, spreading or weathering. No operational or legal attribution is supported.
